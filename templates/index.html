How to play

  1. Create a room and pick a category: General Knowledge, History, Geography, Music, IT, or a mix of all five.
  2. Share the room code (or the invite link) with up to 7 friends.
  3. The question appears one word at a time. Buzz (or press Space) as soon as you know the answer.
  4. Spell the answer by choosing the right letter from four options, one letter at a time (keys 1–4).
  5. Correct: +10. Wrong or too slow: −5, and you're out for that question — the others can still buzz.
